Volatility = annualised standard deviation of daily log returns over the window (×√252), in percent. ATR20 % = 20-day average true range as a percent of the last close. IV 30d = at-the-money implied volatility interpolated to 30 days from the two nearest listed option expiries (median of ATM calls and puts). IV/RV = IV 30d ÷ 20d realized vol. Only live two-sided quotes traded in the last 14 days are used; a ~ marks a thin chain (few contracts or calls/puts disagree) and those rank last in IV views. Prices are dividend/split adjusted (Yahoo Finance). Universe from Wikipedia's S&P 500 list. Not investment advice.
