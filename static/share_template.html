Realized volatility is the annualised standard deviation of daily log returns over the window, times the square root of 252, in percent. ATR % is the 20-day average true range as a percent of the last close. IV 30d is at-the-money implied volatility interpolated in variance to 30 days from the two listed expiries bracketing that horizon, taking the median of the at-the-money calls and puts. Only live two-sided quotes traded within 14 days are used, and a ~ marks a thin chain where few contracts qualified or the calls and puts disagreed. Those rank last in the two implied-volatility views. IV / RV above 1.00 means options are pricing more movement than the stock has recently delivered.
